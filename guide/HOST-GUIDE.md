# SG500 TABLES: Host Guide

A short guide for TABLE facilitators and hosts who don't have time to read everything.
It covers process and logistics only. Open-specific content, partner lists and speaker details stay in the private host pack and aren't in this public repo.
Source tags in brackets: **FS** = the TABLES Fact Sheet (Google Doc), **TDA** = the "Ten Days of Awe" framing note, **Papers** = the seven Open concept papers. Pulled 2026-09-23. Check the live docs before relying on a time.

## 4. The discernment process (four phases) [FS]
1. **Right posture.** Lay down reputation, the need to be heard, and old wounds.
2. **Discern privately.** Individually, before God, before any discussion.
3. **Sensemake corporately.** A word is *held*, not acted on. Look for **2–3 witnesses** who resonate (2 Cor 13:1; 1 Cor 14:29). No resonance? Set it aside without striving. Expect either a *shared emphasis* or a *composite picture*.
4. **Align corporately.** Regather and notice patterns, overlaps and tensions. It isn't linear, so go back to listening if needed.

**The one rule that matters most:** keep three things apart: *what I sensed* / *what I think it means* / *what it might mean for us*. [FS]

## 5. Five-minute pre-read card [FS]
- **Open with worship, prayer or Scripture** before any discussion.
- Say: *"We have two separate things to discern today: what God is doing in Asia, and what He wants us to do at SG500."*
- **Watch for:** who hasn't spoken; impressions stated as facts; sliding into strategy before listening.
- **Stuck:** "What did you sense, even if it feels small or unclear?"
- **Someone dominating:** "Thank you. Let's hear from a few others before we respond to that."
- **Disagreement:** "Let's capture both. We don't need everyone to agree tonight."
- **Jumping to strategy:** "Let's come back to what we're sensing before we plan." Park the idea.
- **A prophetic impression:** slow down, ask what was actually seen or heard, test it, and record it as "we sense…", not "God says…".
- **Before you end:** is the working tab actually filled in?

**You ARE responsible for:** spiritual space, protecting the process, everyone contributing, managing dominant voices, separating receiving from interpreting, testing, preventing premature strategy, capturing outputs *and* unresolved questions, and landing next steps.
**You are NOT responsible for:** deciding what God is saying, forcing consensus, defending your preferred outcome, making it all fit neatly, assuming the Asia discernment must dictate the Forum segment, or eliminating uncertainty. [FS]

## 6. Picking a format for your Forum segment [FS]
- **Plenary:** one voice teaches or casts vision. **Panel:** differing lived perspectives tested in real time. **Workshop:** people leave with a practised skill or tool. **Group prayer:** the room responds to God. **Ministry/activation:** personal response or commissioning. **Hybrid:** only if there's time for both.
- **Panel questions:** why a panel? Which perspectives? Who moderates? What questions? How does the audience engage? What happens after?
- **Workshop formats** (2 hrs, run twice):
  1. **Challenge-to-Action Lab:** a real problem → initiative, founding team, first 30-day step.
  2. **Case Clinic:** an existing effort that's stuck → a revised strategy.
  3. **Collaboration Design Studio:** several actors → a joint pilot with named roles.
  4. **Rapid Experiment Lab:** an untested idea → the smallest test, with an owner and metrics.
  5. **Scenario & Strategy Lab:** an uncertain future → no-regret actions.
- Post-its and paper are provided. Anything else, ask **Joshua** early. [FS]

## 7. FAQ
**Q: What exactly am I hosting?** One of 7 TABLEs (~8 people) for your Open. You facilitate discernment and make sure your Open's Forum panel, workshop and evening contributions are designed and written down by Sunday. [FS]

**Q: Is this a strategy meeting?** No. It is framed as a listening gathering. Strategy talk comes last, in the "segment design" block, and even then it should trace to what was discerned. [FS, Papers]

**Q: What do I have to produce?** Sections A–E of your Open's sub-tab, the Forum Evenings column, and a named presenter. See §2. [FS]

**Q: Do I pick the Forum speakers?** Mostly no. Most speakers and some panelists are already selected. You shape the panel's message, flow and any open seats. Check the programming sheet and **Tim Wong**. [FS]

**Q: How long is my Forum session?** The Fact Sheet says each Open session is a 25-min plenary + 45-min panel. The actual programme slots vary by Open. **Design to the programme and confirm with Tim Wong.** [FS]

**Q: What about the workshop?** You design and scope it. It's 2 hrs, run **twice** (Forum Day 2 and Day 3) so people can attend two different workshops. Expect ~40 people per Open. [FS]

**Q: When are the workshop slots?** Mon 28 Sep 14:45–17:00 and Tue 29 Sep 15:15–17:30 ("Workshops & Unconference"). [FS]

**Q: Who do I ask about logistics?** **Joshua**, as early as possible. [FS]

**Q: Who takes notes?** Assign a **scribe** at the Saturday 13:15 framing. The support team also captures insights, handles food and drinks, and takes photos. [FS]

**Q: How do we handle food?** Hosts are encouraged to order **GrabFood** at lunch to save time. *Who pays or claims isn't stated. Ask.* [FS]

**Q: Someone keeps dominating. What do I say?** "Thank you. Let's hear from a few others before we respond to that." Then actively draw out whoever hasn't spoken. [FS]

**Q: People are jumping to solutions.** "Let's come back to what we're sensing before we plan." Park the idea for the design block. [FS]

**Q: Someone shares "a word from God."** Slow down. Ask what was actually seen or heard, invite 2–3 others to test it, and record it humbly ("we sense…"). [FS]

**Q: What if nobody agrees?** That's fine. Capture both. Minority views are data. "We don't need everyone to agree tonight." [FS]

**Q: What if nothing connects to the Forum?** "No connection discerned" is a complete answer for the Asia discernment. The Forum segment is its own track, framed at 17:00 Saturday. [FS]

**Q: Must the Asia discernment determine our Forum segment?** No. The Fact Sheet explicitly says not to assume Track 1 determines Track 2. [FS]

**Q: What if our honest answer is "not settled yet"?** Say so. Every paper's "output constraint" says the Forum slot should reflect what was actually discerned, not paper over it. [Papers]

**Q: We're asked "how much will it cost." Do we need a number?** Capture direction and assumptions. One paper argues the dollar figure should be discerned after the Forum, as a range with stated assumptions. [FS, Papers]

**Q: What's Kyrios / Kairos / Kainos / Koinonia?** Lordship (flags buried, one banner raised) / the season we're in / the new wineskin God is making / fellowship as one body. [Papers]

**Q: What are LTG, LTT, LTH?** LTG: 2–3 people weekly (Scripture, confession, prayer for named people). LTT: a Table of 8–12 exploring faith. LTH: a hub open to a whole neighbourhood or workplace. [Papers]

**Q: What's BLESS?** Begin with prayer for five named people, Listen, Eat, Serve, Share. [FS]

**Q: Are missional communities churches?** No. They are bridging communities and low-threshold on-ramps. New believers are connected to a local church. [FS]

**Q: Why Singapore?** It's the testbed. 82% don't yet follow Jesus. What works here is designed to travel to 500 Asian cities by 2033. [FS]

**Q: What happens Sunday?** Worship on Koinonia, each Open shares its Koinonia answer and its segments (~20 min), then everyone shapes Forum Day 3 night and the CTA. Close by 17:00, then dinner and the Forum. [FS]

**Q: What's already confirmed for the Call to Action?** Launch testbeds in your city (SG500); the Movement Day Scholars Program; Indigitous (TBD); annualising the Forum (TBD). [FS]

**Q: What came out of the 2024 Forum that I should build on?** Relationships first. Avoid transactionality. Panel then prayer response, unconference breakouts, and shared meals worked well. [2024 report]
